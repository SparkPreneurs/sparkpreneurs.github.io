What They'll Explore

  • Hands-on experiments that teach scientific observation and curiosity.
  • Engineering challenges that turn ideas into real-world builds.
  • Intro coding and logic puzzles that sharpen problem-solving skills.

Perfect For

  • Curious kids who love asking how and why things work.
  • Builders and tinkerers who enjoy hands-on challenges.
  • Learners who want a fun way to explore math and technology.

Program Extras

  • Take-home challenge cards to keep the curiosity going.
  • Mini demo days that celebrate projects and teamwork.
  • Small-group coaching to support steady progress.

Prep & Reminders

  • No prior experience required. We supply the materials.
  • Bring a water bottle and closed-toe shoes for builds.
  • Ask about take-home project kits if your child wants more STEM time.
