Music Club

Play. Sing. Perform.

Music Club blends rhythm games, vocal play, and beginner instruments to help kids find their sound and share it proudly.

Rhythm Adventures Band Jam Sessions Showtime Confidence

From boomwhackers and ukuleles to movement-based percussion, every class ends with a mini performance moment.

What They’ll Explore

  • Beat-building games that teach tempo, rhythm, and listening skills.
  • Group singing, harmonies, and vocal warm-ups that boost stage presence.
  • Instrument rotations featuring percussion, keyboards, ukuleles, and more.

Perfect For

  • Movers and shakers who love to clap, sing, and celebrate sound.
  • Kids who want a low-pressure way to try different instruments.
  • Performers preparing for school concerts, talent shows, or auditions.

Program Extras

  • Songbooks and practice playlists sent home after each session.
  • Opportunities to perform at SparkPreneurs community showcases.
  • Small-group coaching to help each musician find their voice.

Prep & Reminders

  • No instruments required—we supply everything!
  • Bring a water bottle and comfy shoes for movement breaks.
  • Ask about private lessons or duet pods if your child wants more music time.
