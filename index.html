About SparkPreneurs

More Than an Art Studio

SparkPreneurs is a community where art, wellness, creativity, and entrepreneurial thinking come together.

Located in the heart of Midtown Toronto, we welcome children, teens, and adults to discover new passions, develop creative skills, and build confidence in a warm, inspiring environment.

Whether you're creating your first painting, exploring pottery, joining a dance or yoga class, learning a new creative technology, or celebrating a special occasion, SparkPreneurs is a place where ideas come to life and people connect through meaningful experiences.
