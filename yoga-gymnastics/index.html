Yoga & Gymnastics

Stretch. Move. Shine.

Yoga & Gymnastics blends mindful movement with playful strength training so every child gains balance, flexibility, and body awareness.

Mindful Flow Balance Circuits Confidence Boosts

Classes rotate through yoga stories, tumbling circuits, partner poses, and calming cooldowns to keep the energy joyful and focused.

What They’ll Explore

  • Yoga stories that teach mindful breathing, posture, and focus.
  • Tumbling skills including rolls, handstands, and safe spotting techniques.
  • Strength and flexibility games that build coordination and resilience.

Perfect For

  • High-energy kids who love to move, stretch, and try new skills.
  • Children seeking calm confidence before sports, dance, or school events.
  • Friends who want an uplifting way to stay active together.

Program Extras

  • Progress ribbons and mindful journals to track new poses.
  • Parent tips for at-home stretching and calming routines.
  • Invitations to seasonal family movement sessions.

Prep & Reminders

  • Bring a water bottle and tie back long hair for safety.
  • Wear comfortable activewear—bare feet welcome on the mats.
  • Please share any mobility notes so our coaches can personalize support.
