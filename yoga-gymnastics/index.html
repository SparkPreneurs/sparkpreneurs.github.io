Yoga & Dance

Stretch. Dance. Shine.

Yoga & Dance blends mindful movement with joyful rhythm so every participant gains balance, coordination, creativity, and confidence.

Mindful Flow Dance + Rhythm Confidence Boosts

Classes rotate through yoga stories, upbeat dance combinations, movement games, and calming cooldowns to keep the energy joyful and focused.

What They’ll Explore

  • Yoga stories that teach mindful breathing, posture, and focus.
  • Dance combinations that build rhythm, coordination, and creative expression.
  • Movement games that strengthen flexibility, teamwork, and confidence.

Perfect For

  • High-energy participants who love to move, stretch, and try new skills.
  • People seeking calm confidence and a joyful way to express themselves.
  • Friends who want an uplifting way to stay active together.

Program Extras

  • Mindful journals and celebration moments to track new poses and dance skills.
  • At-home tips for stretching and calming routines.
  • Invitations to seasonal family movement sessions.

Prep & Reminders

  • Bring a water bottle and tie back long hair for safety.
  • Wear comfortable activewear—bare feet welcome on the mats.
  • Please share any mobility notes so our coaches can personalize support.
