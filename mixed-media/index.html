The Class

Create Without Limits

Mixed Media Art gives children the freedom to explore different materials and techniques within the same creative project. Participants may combine painting, drawing, collage, crafts, texture work, and clay elements to create original artwork while developing confidence, imagination, and creative problem-solving skills.
