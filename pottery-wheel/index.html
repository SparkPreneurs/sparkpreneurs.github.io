Program Overview

Create on the Wheel

Explore the creative and calming experience of pottery wheel throwing in a small, supportive class. Participants will learn essential techniques for centring clay, shaping forms, refining their work, and creating functional or decorative pottery pieces. No prior experience is required.

Course Materials

What’s Included

  • All clay, tools, and class materials
  • Step-by-step instructor guidance
  • Glazing and kiln firing for one finished piece
  • Use of pottery wheels during class
  • Studio cleanup and basic finishing support

Glazing and kiln firing for one selected piece are included in the course fee. Additional pieces may be glazed and kiln-fired for an extra fee.

Skills and Techniques

What You’ll Learn

  • Preparing clay
  • Centring clay on the wheel
  • Opening and shaping a form
  • Creating cylinders, bowls, or simple vessels
  • Controlling wall thickness
  • Trimming and refining
  • Basic surface decoration
  • Preparing one piece for glazing and firing
  • Safe use and care of pottery tools
  • Building confidence through practice

Finished Pieces

From Clay to Finished Piece

During the four-session course, participants will create and refine pottery on the wheel. One selected piece will be glazed and professionally kiln-fired as part of the course fee. Additional pieces can be glazed and fired for an extra fee.

Finished work is professionally kiln-fired through our ceramic firing service. Kiln firing requires additional processing time. Pickup timing for completed pieces will be shared during the course.
