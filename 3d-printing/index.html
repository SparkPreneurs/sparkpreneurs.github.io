Program Overview

Explore. Design. Create.

Children will explore the exciting world of 3D printing by learning basic design concepts, creating digital models, and watching their ideas come to life. Through fun, project-based learning, participants will develop creativity, problem-solving skills, confidence, and an understanding of modern design technology.

The Experience

From Idea to Finished Creation

Participants will move through the creative process from brainstorming and digital design to preparing and producing a finished 3D-printed project. Each session will combine guided instruction with hands-on experimentation and creative problem-solving.
