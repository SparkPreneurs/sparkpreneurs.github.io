Robotics Lab

Imagine. Code. Engineer.

Robotics Lab combines LEGO-based builds with beginner coding so kids can prototype, test, and celebrate their inventions.

LEGO Engineering Coding Challenges Team Showcases

Guided missions, design journals, and plenty of iteration help makers think like engineers while keeping play at the center.

Reserve Your Spot

Write “Robotics Lab” in your note so we can recommend the best level for your child.

What They’ll Explore

  • Engineering builds that focus on gears, sensors, and simple machines.
  • Block-based coding challenges that introduce loops, logic, and debugging.
  • Innovation sprints where teams pitch, prototype, and test solutions.

Perfect For

  • Tinkerers who love to build, take apart, and rebuild again.
  • Gamers curious about how technology and coding really work.
  • Kids preparing for STEM fairs, robotics clubs, or innovation showcases.

Program Extras

  • Engineering notebooks to track ideas, sketches, and code snippets.
  • Parent progress recaps with suggested at-home challenges.
  • Opportunities to join SparkPreneurs robotics competition squads.

Prep & Reminders

  • Bring a water bottle—brainpower needs fuel!
  • Closed-toe shoes recommended for build days.
  • Have a project idea? Share it and we’ll help your child bring it to life.
