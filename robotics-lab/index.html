3D Printing

Design. Model. Print.

3D Printing helps creators of all ages turn imaginative ideas into real objects through beginner-friendly digital design, modeling, and printing.

Beginner 3D Design Creative Prototypes Printed Projects

Guided design challenges help makers plan, build, test, and improve their models while learning how 3D printers bring them to life.

What They’ll Explore

  • Beginner-friendly 3D modeling tools for shapes, objects, and characters.
  • Design thinking that turns sketches and ideas into printable models.
  • Prototyping skills through testing, improving, and printing creations.

Perfect For

  • Creative thinkers who love inventing and building new things.
  • People curious about digital art, design, and modern technology.
  • Makers who want to see their ideas become real objects.

Program Extras

  • Design journals to track ideas, sketches, and model improvements.
  • Printed creations that participants can proudly take home.
  • Mini showcases where makers share the stories behind their designs.

Prep & Reminders

  • No prior 3D design experience is required.
  • Bring a water bottle and plenty of creative ideas.
  • Printing takes time, so some finished projects may go home later.
