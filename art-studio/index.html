Art Studio

Paint. Build. Create.

Our Art Studio program invites creators of all ages to experiment with color, texture, and bold ideas in a joyful, encouraging studio environment.

Mixed Media Projects Creative Confidence Mini Gallery Shows

Each week introduces a new focus, from watercolor stories and printmaking to clay sculpture and collaborative murals.

What They’ll Explore

  • Weekly themed lessons that blend painting, printmaking, sculpture, and collage.
  • Technique mini-demos that build fine motor skills and creative problem-solving.
  • Collaborative projects that encourage teamwork and positive feedback.

Perfect For

  • Curious makers who light up when they see a rainbow of art supplies.
  • Creators who love storytelling, design, and transforming ideas into masterpieces.
  • Artists seeking a confidence boost in a supportive, upbeat studio space.

Program Extras

  • All premium supplies and aprons provided by SparkPreneurs.
  • Progress snapshots emailed to families after each project cycle.
  • Invites to quarterly gallery nights featuring participant artwork.

Prep & Reminders

  • Arrive 5 minutes early to get settled and choose a station.
  • Dress for joyful mess—paint shirts are encouraged!
  • Need to swap a class? Reach out and we’ll help with a make-up session.
